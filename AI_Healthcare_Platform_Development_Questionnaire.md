## QUESTIONNAIRE ADMINISTRATION NOTES

### Target Audience
- **Healthcare Providers**: Doctors, nurses, community health workers
- **Patients/Community Members**: End users of the platform
- **Administrators**: Hospital administrators, government health officials
- **Technical Staff**: IT professionals, system administrators
- **Community Leaders**: Religious leaders, traditional healers, local officials

### Distribution Strategy
1. **In-person interviews** for detailed responses
2. **Online surveys** for broader reach
3. **Focus groups** for community discussions
4. **Workshops** for stakeholder engagement

### Analysis Framework
1. **Quantitative Analysis**: Closed-ended question responses
2. **Qualitative Analysis**: Open-ended question themes
3. **Stakeholder Mapping**: Different perspectives and needs
4. **Priority Ranking**: Most important features and concerns
5. **Risk Assessment**: Identified risks and mitigation strategies

### Follow-up Actions
1. **Requirements Documentation**: Based on questionnaire responses
2. **System Design**: Incorporating stakeholder feedback
3. **Prototype Development**: Testing key features
4. **Pilot Testing**: Community validation
5. **Iterative Improvement**: Continuous feedback integration

*This questionnaire is designed to be comprehensive yet flexible. Adapt questions based on specific stakeholder groups and local context. The goal is to ensure the AI Healthcare Platform meets real-world needs and can be successfully implemented in resource-limited settings.*
